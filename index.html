Varun A. Paturkar

PhD Student, Computer Science and Engineering · CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad

I grew up collecting toy cars, and somewhere along the way I got more curious about how real cars work than how they look. That led me to mechanical engineering, and from there to teaching cars to see.

I'm now a PhD student at CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad, working with Prof. C. V. Jawahar on making autonomous driving safer in messy, real-world traffic, especially for two-wheelers. Before this, I worked on place recognition with Prof. Rahul Kala at IIIT Allahabad, and spent a few years building vision systems in industry, including a crowd-safety study with Prof. Dinesh Manocha.

When I'm not thinking about traffic, I'm probably watching live sports or discussing them: cricket, the NBA, the NFL, UFC, and a list that keeps growing.
